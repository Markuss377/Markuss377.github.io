Future Goals

My plans for further study, work experience and future IT roles.

Future goal commentary: This page explains the type of further study I want to make after my current course and how I can work towards it.

My Main Goal

My main goal is to continue developing my IT skills so I can move towards further study, an apprenticeship or an entry-level IT role in the future. I want to build more and improving the quality of my projects.

How I Plan to Get There

  • Complete my IT course with the best grades I can
  • Continue studying IT at a higher level such as Level 3
  • Gain work experience through an apprenticeship, volunteering or a part-time job
  • Keep improving my technical skills in my own time and at college
  • Use feedback to improve my portfolio and others projects

Future Improvements to This Portfolio
